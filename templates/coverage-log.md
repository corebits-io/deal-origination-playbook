## The coverage question

At any point, you should be able to answer in one sentence:

> "We have enumerated roughly X of an estimated Y companies in the [segment]
> segment of [sector] in [geography], from Z of the N sources on the map, and the
> biggest untouched gap is [gap]."

If you cannot say that sentence, the log is out of date — and "it feels like we
have covered it" is exactly the belief this file exists to disprove.
